- available: Part is available for use. Cannot be true if the part is already placed/used.
- free: Location is unoccupied.
- at: Part has been placed at location, but its assembly is not necessarily complete.
- tightened: The screw is tightened.
- installedAt: Part is fully assembled at location, including its protective hoods.
- connected: Static predicate indicating linking hood and screw locations as each screw is covered by a hood.
